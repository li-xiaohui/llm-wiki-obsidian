# Nvidia (NVDA) — Investment Thesis: LONG

## Elevator Pitch

Nvidia is the dominant AI compute platform generating $216B in annual revenue (FY2026) with 90%+ AI accelerator market share, a CUDA software moat measured in years of switching cost, and a $725B hyperscaler capex wave still building behind it. The stock trades at ~24x forward earnings ahead of a May 20 earnings report where consensus expects $78.8B revenue (+77% YoY). China is lost — but already priced in at 5% of revenue. The Vera Rubin platform (H2 2026) represents the next $100B+ product cycle. The networking business grew 263% last quarter to $11B, creating a second growth engine. The bear case (custom ASICs eroding share) is real but slow-moving — Nvidia has 3+ years of roadmap visibility and the most entrenched software ecosystem in computing history.

### Valuation Assessment

The trailing P/E of ~47x appears elevated, but the forward P/E of ~24x on 77% revenue growth gives a PEG ratio of ~0.55 — cheap for a company growing this fast with 60%+ gross margins and an effective monopoly. The key question is sustainability of growth into FY2028+. If revenue growth decelerates to 30-40% in FY2028 (as ASIC competition ramps), the forward multiple compresses to ~30-35x — still reasonable for the AI platform leader.

## 3. AI COMPUTE PLATFORM DOMINANCE

### Market Position

- ~90% market share in AI accelerators (training)
- ~70-80% of total AI accelerator market (including inference)
- CUDA software ecosystem = years of switching cost for developers
- 4M+ developers on CUDA platform
- Every major foundation model (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama) built on Nvidia GPUs

### The CUDA Moat

CUDA is not just a programming language — it's an ecosystem of:
- Libraries (cuDNN, TensorRT, NCCL)
- Frameworks integration (PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX all CUDA-native)
- Developer tools and profilers
- Years of optimization for specific workloads
- Enterprise support and deployment tooling

Switching cost is measured in years of engineering investment, not hardware cost. Even hyperscalers building custom ASICs maintain large Nvidia GPU fleets for flexibility and new workload development.

### Networking: The Second Growth Engine

Nvidia's networking business is quietly becoming a multi-billion-dollar behemoth:
- Q4 FY2026: $11B revenue (+263% YoY)
- Full FY2026: $31B+ (+142% YoY)
- Products: NVLink, InfiniBand, Spectrum-X Ethernet
- InfiniBand revenue nearly doubled sequentially in Q4
- Networking is critical for multi-GPU scale-up and scale-out training

This diversifies Nvidia beyond pure GPU sales and deepens its data center lock-in. Every NVLink-connected Blackwell rack makes it harder to swap in competing accelerators.

Source: [Motley Fool - Networking 263%](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/03/26/nvidias-networking-grew-263-ai-trade-gpus/), [AI Business Review](https://www.aibusinessreview.org/2026/03/19/nvidia-networking-11-billion-revenue-quarterly/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/18/nvidia-networking-division-building-a-multibillion-dollar-behemoth-to-rival-its-chips-business/)

Source: [Nvidia Newsroom - Vera Rubin](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-vera-rubin-platform), [VideoCardz](https://videocardz.com/newz/nvidia-vera-rubin-nvl72-detailed-72-gpus-36-cpus-260-tb-s-scale-up-bandwidth), [Tech-Insider](https://tech-insider.org/nvidia-gtc-2026-rubin-gpu-analysis/)

- Announced: Computex 2024, detailed at CES 2026

- Designed for "agentic AI" — massive-context inference at scale
- Also launched Rubin CPX: a new GPU class designed for massive-context inference

### Revenue Implications

Vera Rubin represents the next $100B+ product cycle. Each platform generation has expanded Nvidia's TAM. Blackwell went from initial $10B quarterly run-rate to $50B+. Rubin, at 2.5x performance, enables new workloads (agentic AI, reasoning) that justify continued capex expansion.

## 6. CHINA SITUATION (ALREADY PRICED IN)

### Revenue Impact

- China revenue: ~5% of total (down from 26% in FY2022)
- H200: Approved for sale by US government, zero units sold (China blocking)
- Q1 FY2026: $4.5B charge on H20 inventory writedown
- FY2027 guidance: explicitly excludes all China Data Center compute revenue

### Why This Is Manageable

- China effectively at zero already — no further downside from here
- $4.5B H20 writedown was a one-time charge (Q1 FY2026)
- Remaining 95% of revenue growing 77%+ YoY
- Ex-China growth is what matters — and it's accelerating
- China lost its leverage: Nvidia doesn't need China for growth

### Long-Term Risk

- China's domestic chip ecosystem (Huawei) may eventually compete globally
- But currently 3-5 years behind on process technology and ecosystem
- Memory (HBM) remains a bottleneck China hasn't solved domestically

**Key data points:**
- Trainium 3 offers 30-40% price-performance improvement over GPUs
- Frontier AI companies (Anthropic, OpenAI) running workloads on Trainium 3
- Kearney projects Nvidia AI market share declines from 85% → 70% by 2030
- Inference market share could fall from 90%+ to 20-30% by 2028

Source: [Oplexa - Trainium vs Blackwell](https://oplexa.com/amazon-trainium-3-vs-nvidia-blackwell/), [Introl Blog](https://introl.com/blog/custom-silicon-inflection-2026-hyperscaler-asics-nvidia-gpu)

### Why ASICs Don't Kill Nvidia

1. **TAM is expanding faster than share is eroding**: If AI compute TAM grows 5x but Nvidia's share falls from 90% to 70%, revenue still grows 3.9x
2. **GPUs remain essential for R&D/new workloads**: Even hyperscalers with custom ASICs keep large GPU fleets for flexibility
3. **CUDA switching cost**: Moving production inference to ASICs takes years per model
4. **Networking lock-in**: NVLink/InfiniBand connectivity makes the rack the unit of competition, not the chip
5. **Roadmap cadence**: Annual architecture generations (Blackwell → Rubin → Rubin Ultra → next) keep performance leadership

## 8. BULL CASE SUMMARY

1. **AI compute is still early innings**: $725B hyperscaler capex in 2026, accelerating. Agentic AI, reasoning, and enterprise adoption are just beginning. TAM expansion outpaces share erosion.

2. **Vera Rubin extends leadership**: 2.5x Blackwell performance on 3nm. HBM4 memory. NVL72 scale-up. Launches H2 2026 — the next $100B+ product cycle with no credible competitor at this performance tier.

3. **CUDA moat is widening, not shrinking**: 4M+ developers, every framework, every enterprise tool. Networking ($31B, +142%) deepens the lock-in at the rack level.

4. **Forward P/E of ~24x is cheap for 77% growth**: PEG of ~0.55. Nvidia generates $25B+ FCF per quarter with $1-2B capex. The fabless model is an extraordinary cash machine.

5. **China is priced in at zero**: Q1 FY2027 guidance explicitly excludes China. No further downside from export controls. The 95% ex-China business is accelerating.

6. **Capital returns accelerating**: $71B buyback authorization, 50% FCF commitment, $25B+/quarter FCF = $50B+ annual returns to shareholders.

## 11. THESIS KILL CONDITIONS

Exit or fundamentally re-evaluate if:

1. **Q2 FY2027 guidance below $82B** — would signal growth deceleration faster than expected
2. **Two or more hyperscalers cut AI capex guidance** — undermines the entire demand thesis
3. **Gross margins fall below 55% for two consecutive quarters** — would indicate pricing power loss to ASICs
4. **AMD or custom ASICs win majority of a major hyperscaler's new capacity** (e.g., Google moves >50% training to TPU, or Microsoft shifts majority to custom) — would confirm moat erosion
5. **Vera Rubin production delay >6 months** — would open window for competitors
6. **Nvidia P/E expands above 60x with no acceleration in growth** — would signal bubble/euphoria

## 12. POSITION SIZING FRAMEWORK

**Entry timing considerations:**
- May 20 earnings is a binary event — consider sizing around it
- Historical pattern: Nvidia tends to beat and raise, but reactions depend on magnitude of Q2 guide
- If Q2 guide matches/beats $86B consensus: stock likely rips higher
- If Q2 guide disappoints even slightly: could pull back 10-15% (opportunity)

**Suggested approach:**
- Core position pre-earnings (conviction in structural thesis)
- Reserve capital for potential post-earnings pullback if guide disappoints
- Position size appropriate for a $5.7T market cap stock with 3-5% daily moves common around earnings

## CONVICTION: HIGH

The combination of 77% revenue growth, ~24x forward P/E, $725B in confirmed hyperscaler capex, China already priced at zero, $25B+ quarterly FCF, and a next-generation platform launching in H2 2026 makes this a high-conviction long. The custom ASIC threat is real but the TAM is expanding faster than share is eroding. May 20 earnings is the next key data point.
