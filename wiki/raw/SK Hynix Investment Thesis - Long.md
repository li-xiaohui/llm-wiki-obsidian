# SK Hynix (000660.KS) — Investment Thesis: LONG

## Elevator Pitch

SK Hynix is the undisputed king of HBM — the most critical bottleneck in AI infrastructure. With 62% HBM market share, 70% of Nvidia's HBM4 orders for Vera Rubin, a record 72% operating margin, and customer demand exceeding supply capacity for the next three years, this is a structural monopoly on AI memory priced at ~6x forward earnings. The stock has rallied 200%+ in 2026 but remains cheap on forward metrics because the earnings trajectory is exponential. A US ADR listing in H2 2026 ($10-14B raise) will unlock a new investor base. Samsung is catching up on HBM4 but SK Hynix's first-mover advantage with Nvidia — the world's most important chip customer — is a moat that widens with each product generation.

### Valuation Assessment

At a forward P/E of ~6x, SK Hynix trades at an 84% discount to the semiconductor industry median (37x). This is despite:
- 72% operating margins (industry-leading)
- 198% revenue growth
- 62% market share in the fastest-growing segment of semiconductors

The market is pricing in cyclicality — that these earnings are unsustainable. But management says demand exceeds supply through at least 2028. If even 50% of current earnings persist, the stock is absurdly cheap.

## 3. HBM DOMINANCE — THE CORE THESIS

### Market Share

| Period | SK Hynix | Samsung | Micron |
|--------|----------|---------|--------|
| Q2 2025 | 62% | 17% | 21% |
| Q3 2025 | 53% | 35% | 11% |
| 2026E (HBM4) | 54-70% | 28-30% | 10-18% |

Source: [Astute Group](https://www.astutegroup.com/news/general/sk-hynix-holds-62-of-hbm-micron-overtakes-samsung-2026-battle-pivots-to-hbm4/)

### Nvidia Relationship (Critical Moat)

SK Hynix is Nvidia's primary HBM supplier — the most important customer relationship in semiconductors:

- **HBM4**: SK Hynix secured ~70% of Nvidia's HBM4 orders for Vera Rubin (vs. Samsung ~30%)
- **HBM3E**: Also supplying to Microsoft for Maia 200 custom chip
- **Validation**: HBM4 paid samples cleared Nvidia's final validation without issues
- **Mass production**: Full-scale HBM4 production underway to meet Nvidia's H2 2026 schedule
- **Multi-year visibility**: Nvidia is SK Hynix's anchor customer with demand exceeding capacity for 3+ years

Source: [TrendForce - Two-Thirds Nvidia HBM4](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/01/28/news-sk-hynix-reportedly-to-supply-about-two-thirds-of-nvidia-hbm4-samsung-targets-early-delivery/), [Semicone - 70% Nvidia HBM4](https://www.semicone.com/article-385.html), [KED Global](https://www.kedglobal.com/korean-chipmakers/newsView/ked202601280002)

### Why HBM Share Is Sticky

1. **First-mover advantage**: SK Hynix was first to qualify HBM3E with Nvidia, first to mass-produce HBM4
2. **Yield leadership**: Consistently ahead of Samsung on HBM yields
3. **Co-development**: Deep integration with Nvidia's architecture team — each GPU generation is co-designed with SK Hynix HBM specifications
4. **Switching cost**: Changing HBM supplier mid-cycle risks qualification delays, yield issues, and production timeline slips — unacceptable for a $5.7T company's flagship product
5. **Capacity commitment**: SK Hynix has already sold out 2026 HBM supply

## 6. BULL CASE SUMMARY

1. **HBM monopoly position**: 62% market share, 70% of Nvidia's HBM4 orders, sold out through 2026, demand exceeding capacity for 3+ years. This is the most important memory product in the world and SK Hynix controls it.

2. **Record-shattering financials**: 72% operating margin (global chip industry record), 198% revenue growth, tripling revenue in a single year. Q1 2026 OP alone was KRW 37.6T.

3. **Absurdly cheap on forward earnings**: Forward P/E of ~6x for a company with 72% margins, 198% growth, and a structural monopoly. 84% discount to semiconductor median. Even if earnings halve, the stock is cheap.

4. **Supply-constrained through 2028**: Management and industry sources confirm memory shortages persist through at least 2027-2028. Pricing power has shifted to suppliers. DRAM prices +47% in 2026.

5. **US ADR listing catalyst**: $10-14B raise in H2 2026 unlocks massive new investor base. Currently inaccessible to most US retail investors.

6. **Nvidia relationship deepens**: HBM4 co-designed for Vera Rubin. Each generation strengthens the bond. Switching costs are asymmetric — Nvidia needs SK Hynix more than SK Hynix needs any single customer (though Nvidia is by far the largest).

## 7. KEY RISKS

| Risk | Severity | Probability | Timeline | Detail |
|------|----------|-------------|----------|--------|
| Samsung HBM4 catch-up | HIGH | Medium | H2 2026+ | Samsung passed HBM4 validation with Nvidia/AMD; full-scale supply from June. Could erode SK Hynix share from 70% toward 50%. |
| Memory cycle peak / 2027 oversupply | HIGH | Medium | 2027-2028 | SK Hynix quietly cut HBM-specific capex in 2025 due to internal oversupply modelling for 2027-2028. Capex decisions now become wafers in 2027-2028. |
| Pure-play concentration risk | MEDIUM | Ongoing | — | Unlike Samsung (diversified), SK Hynix is ~95% memory. A memory downturn hits 100% of revenue. |
| Kospi volatility / ETF rebalancing | MEDIUM | Ongoing | — | SK Hynix accounts for ~25% of Kospi. 17% of daily volume from ETF rebalancing. 5%+ daily swings common. |
| Valuation compression on deceleration | MEDIUM | Medium | H2 2026+ | 200%+ rally in 2026 means high expectations embedded. Any growth miss could trigger sharp correction. |
| HBM commoditization | MEDIUM | Low (near-term) | 2028+ | If HBM becomes standardized commodity (like DRAM), margins compress from 72% toward industry norms (30-40%). Bear case target: KRW 130,000. |
| US ADR dilution | LOW | Medium | H2 2026 | $10-14B raise could dilute existing shareholders if not offset by buybacks. |

### Samsung Catch-Up: The Key Competitive Risk

Samsung has:
- Passed HBM4 final validation with Nvidia and AMD
- Full-scale HBM4 supply expected from June 2026
- Planning 50% HBM capacity surge in 2026
- Recovered HBM share from 17% to 35% in a single quarter (Q2→Q3 2025)

**However**: SK Hynix has first-mover advantage on each HBM generation, deeper Nvidia co-design relationship, and yield leadership. Samsung catching up from 30% to 40% is possible; overtaking SK Hynix in the next 2 years is unlikely.

## 9. THESIS KILL CONDITIONS

Exit or fundamentally re-evaluate if:

1. **Operating margins fall below 50% for two consecutive quarters** — would signal HBM commoditization or pricing power loss
2. **Samsung secures >50% of Nvidia's HBM4 orders** in a re-allocation — would indicate loss of primary supplier status
3. **DRAM spot prices decline for 3 consecutive months** — would signal cycle turning
4. **SK Hynix loses HBM4E/HBM5 first-mover position to Samsung** — would break the generational leadership pattern
5. **Multiple hyperscalers cut AI capex** — undermines demand thesis
6. **US ADR listing cancelled or significantly downsized** — removes key catalyst

## 10. POSITION SIZING FRAMEWORK

**Entry considerations:**
- Stock has rallied 200%+ YTD — momentum is strong but pullbacks are violent (5%+ daily moves)
- The May 15 Kospi crash saw SK Hynix -7.7% in a single session
- Position size must accommodate Korean market volatility
- ADR listing in H2 2026 may offer a more liquid entry point for US-based investors

**Suggested approach:**
- Initial position now at ~$860B market cap / KRW 1,225,000
- Add on any Kospi-driven pullback to KRW 1,100,000 (if fundamentals unchanged)
- Consider waiting for US ADR for more liquid access
- Size for 20-25% max drawdown tolerance given Kospi volatility

---

## CONVICTION: HIGH

Forward P/E of ~6x on a company with 72% operating margins, 62% market share in the AI bottleneck, 70% of Nvidia's HBM4 orders, demand exceeding supply through 2028, and a US ADR listing catalyst in H2 2026. Samsung is catching up but the first-mover advantage compounds with each product generation. The memory cycle peak is a real risk for 2027-2028, but the near-term setup is as strong as any semiconductor company in the world.
