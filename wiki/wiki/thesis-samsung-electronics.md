# Investment Thesis: Samsung Electronics (005930.KS)

**Summary**: Long Samsung Electronics — a $1.18 trillion AI memory supercycle leader with 48x semiconductor profit growth, sold-out HBM supply through 2026, and a forward P/E of ~6x, trading at a post-crash discount after mechanical foreign selling.

**Sources**: Samsung Electronics Investment Thesis - Long.md, South Korea's world-beating stock rally stumbles as global funds sell.md, Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-16

---

## Direction: LONG | Conviction: HIGH

## Elevator Pitch

Samsung is the world's largest memory chipmaker at the centre of an AI supercycle its executives say will create shortages through 2027. The stock fell 8.6% on May 15 on labour fears and foreign momentum selling — not deteriorating fundamentals. Q1 2026 delivered KRW 57.2T operating profit (48x YoY semiconductor growth). Forward P/E is ~6x. HBM supply is sold out. The selloff is asymmetric.

## Valuation

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Market Cap | ~$1.18 trillion |
| P/E (TTM) | 29.3x (138% above 10-year median of 12.3x) |
| P/E (Forward) | ~5.9x |
| Industry Median P/E | 28.1x |

The trailing P/E looks expensive vs. history but the forward P/E of ~6x implies the market doesn't believe earnings are sustainable. If the supercycle persists (management says through 2027), massive upside. (source: Samsung Electronics Investment Thesis - Long.md)

## HBM Position (Recovering Fast)

- Market share: 17% (Q2 2025) → 35% (Q3 2025) → >30% target (2026)
- HBM3E: Qualified with Nvidia (after multiple attempts). Supplies 60%+ of Google TPU HBM3E
- HBM4: Mass production started Feb 2026. >30% of Nvidia's Vera Rubin supply secured
- Entire 2026 HBM output is sold out
- 50% capacity expansion to 250K wafers/month planned by end 2026
- Samsung and SK Hynix planning ~20% HBM3E price hikes

(source: Samsung Electronics Investment Thesis - Long.md)

## Memory Pricing Tailwind

- DRAM prices +30% in Q1 2026, high single-digit growth in Q2
- Gartner forecasts +47% DRAM price increase across 2026
- Management warns shortages persist through at least 2027

## Labour Dispute (Key Near-Term Risk)

- **Threat**: 18-day strike from May 21, ~41,000 workers, potential cost ~$20B
- **Demands**: 15% of OP to bonuses (vs. mgmt offer of 10%), 7% salary increase
- **April 23 rally impact**: 58% foundry production drop, 18% memory production drop (one day)
- **Mitigants**: Seoul government intervened, negotiable gap exists, full strike would hurt customers (Nvidia, hyperscalers), creating pressure to settle

## Shareholder Returns

- 50% FCF returned to shareholders (2024-2026 program)
- KRW 9.8T annual regular dividend
- 82.5% of treasury shares being cancelled in H1 2026 (~KRW 16T value)
- First special dividend in 5 years (205 won added to quarterly 361 won)

## Foreign Ownership (Contrarian Signal)

- 48.9% — 12-year low
- KRW 51.5T ($37.8B) net sold since February (largest since GFC)
- Historically, extreme foreign selling in Korea is a contrarian buy signal

## Geopolitical Edge

Samsung supplies memory to both US hyperscalers and Chinese firms. Memory chips face less restrictive export controls than logic/GPU. China's self-sufficiency push targets logic (Huawei vs Nvidia), not memory. The US-China bifurcation doubles infrastructure demand — Samsung benefits from both sides building. (source: Samsung Electronics Investment Thesis - Long.md, Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md)
