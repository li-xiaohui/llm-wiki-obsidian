# Nvidia in China

**Summary**: Nvidia's AI chip sales to China show signs of a potential breakthrough after several Chinese tech giants were approved to purchase H200 chips, though Beijing's push for domestic alternatives continues.

**Sources**: Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md, Why Nvidia Stock Hit a New All-Time High Today, and Why There's Likely More to Come.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-16

---

## H200 Sales Stalled

In December 2025, President Trump approved Nvidia to sell its H200 AI chip to China. However, as of May 2026, the Chinese government has not greenlighted any purchases and no H200s have been sold. Beijing has instead pushed Chinese companies to rely on homegrown technology from chipmakers such as Huawei. (source: Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md)

## Trump-Xi Summit (May 2026)

Jensen Huang, Nvidia's CEO, joined the group of American business leaders traveling with Trump to Beijing, raising expectations of progress. However:

- The summit yielded no clarity on Nvidia's China future
- US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer said it was "up to Beijing" whether Chinese companies would buy from Nvidia
- Chip export controls were not discussed at the meeting
- Greer called it a "sovereign decision for China"

Trump said on Air Force One that China "chose not to" buy H200s to focus on developing their own chips, but struck an optimistic note: "I think something could happen on that." (source: Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md)

## H200 Purchase Approvals (May 15, 2026)

Reports emerged on May 15 that several high-profile Chinese companies have been approved to purchase Nvidia's H200 AI chips, including Alibaba, Tencent Holdings, ByteDance, and JD.com, according to Reuters. Jensen Huang believes the Chinese AI chip market could be worth as much as $50 billion to Nvidia. This development, combined with the summit, sent Nvidia stock to a new all-time high (+4.7% intraday). (source: Why Nvidia Stock Hit a New All-Time High Today, and Why There's Likely More to Come.md)

**Note**: This contradicts earlier reporting that no H200 purchases had been approved. The key distinction appears to be that the US has approved specific Chinese companies to buy, but China's government has not yet greenlighted the imports. Whether actual sales materialize remains uncertain. [NEEDS MONITORING]

## China's Self-Sufficiency Push

Just before the Trump-Xi summit, China reached a milestone: DeepSeek announced its latest AI model had been optimized to run on Huawei chips — the first time a major Chinese AI model ran on fully domestic hardware.

Jensen Huang had long warned this shift was coming, predicting that China's AI companies would eventually rely on Chinese hardware rather than American technology, eroding US influence over AI development in China. (source: Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md)
