# Investment Thesis: Nvidia (NVDA)

**Summary**: Long Nvidia — the $5.7 trillion AI compute platform monopoly with 90% accelerator share, ~24x forward P/E on 77% revenue growth, $725B hyperscaler capex behind it, and the Vera Rubin platform launching H2 2026.

**Sources**: Nvidia Investment Thesis - Long.md, Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md, Cerebras IPO Ushering in a New Era of AI Hardware.md, Why Nvidia Stock Hit a New All-Time High Today, and Why There's Likely More to Come.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-16

---

## Direction: LONG | Conviction: HIGH

## Elevator Pitch

Nvidia is generating $216B annual revenue with 90%+ AI accelerator market share and a CUDA moat measured in years of switching cost. Forward P/E is ~24x on 77% growth. China is lost (5% of revenue) but already priced in. $725B in hyperscaler capex is committed. Vera Rubin (2.5x Blackwell, HBM4, 336B transistors) launches H2 2026. Networking grew 263% to $11B/quarter — a second growth engine. The ASIC bear case is real but slow; TAM expansion outpaces share erosion.

## Platform Dominance

- 90%+ AI accelerator market share
- CUDA: 4M+ developers, every major framework, years of switching cost
- Networking: $11B/quarter, InfiniBand + NVLink + Spectrum-X create rack-level lock-in
- Every foundation model (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama) built on Nvidia

## Vera Rubin (H2 2026)

- 336B transistors (1.6x Blackwell), TSMC 3nm, dual-die
- HBM4 memory: 288GB/GPU, 22 TB/s bandwidth
- 50 PFLOPS FP4 (2.5x Blackwell), Rubin Ultra: 100 PFLOPS
- NVL72: 72 GPUs, 260 TB/s scale-up
- Primary HBM4 suppliers: Samsung (>30%) + SK Hynix
- Designed for agentic AI / massive-context inference

## Demand: $725B Hyperscaler Capex

Microsoft $190B, Google +63%, AWS +28%, total ~$725B in 2026 — accelerating. Multi-year buildouts. Most spending directed at Nvidia systems. (source: Nvidia Investment Thesis - Long.md)

## China (Potential Upside Optionality)

- Revenue: ~5% (down from 26% in FY2022)
- H200: Zero sales to date, but Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance, JD.com now approved to purchase (Reuters, May 15)
- Jensen Huang values China market at up to $50B for Nvidia — a 23% revenue uplift if re-entered
- FY2027 guidance explicitly excludes China — any sales would be pure upside
- $4.5B H20 writedown was one-time (Q1 FY2026)
- **Shift**: China no longer just "priced in at zero" — there's now upside optionality from approved buyers

(source: Nvidia Investment Thesis - Long.md, [[nvidia-china]], Why Nvidia Stock Hit a New All-Time High Today, and Why There's Likely More to Come.md)

## Key Risks

| Risk | Severity | Timeline |
|------|----------|----------|
| Custom ASICs (Google TPU v8, Amazon Trainium 3) | HIGH | 2-5 years gradual |
| AI capex cycle deceleration | HIGH | 12-18 months |
| Gross margin compression | MEDIUM | 2-3 years |
| Valuation compression if growth slows | MEDIUM | FY2028+ |

**Bear case detail**: Trainium 3 offers 30-40% price-performance advantage. Anthropic/OpenAI running workloads on it. Inference share could fall from 90%+ to 20-30% by 2028. But TAM expanding 5x while share drops from 90% to 70% = revenue still grows 3.9x.
