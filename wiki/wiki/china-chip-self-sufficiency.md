# China Chip Self-Sufficiency

**Summary**: China is pursuing aggressive technological self-sufficiency in AI chips, with Huawei emerging as the domestic alternative to Nvidia and DeepSeek becoming the first major AI model optimized for Chinese hardware.

**Sources**: Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-16

---

## Strategy

Beijing is actively pushing Chinese companies to rely on homegrown chip technology rather than American imports. Despite the US approving Nvidia's H200 for sale to China, the Chinese government has not greenlit any purchases, choosing instead to nurture domestic capabilities. (source: Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md)

## Key Milestone: DeepSeek on Huawei

In May 2026, just before the Trump-Xi summit, Chinese AI startup DeepSeek announced that its latest AI model had been optimized to run on Huawei chips. This was the first time a major Chinese AI model ran on fully domestic hardware — a significant milestone in China's quest for technological self-sufficiency. (source: Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md)

## Huawei's Role

Huawei has emerged as China's primary domestic alternative to Nvidia for AI compute. Beijing has directed Chinese companies toward Huawei's chips even when Western alternatives are theoretically available. (source: Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md)

## US Perspective

- US Trade Representative Greer acknowledged it is China's "sovereign decision"
- China views the US tech industry as a threat to domestic growth
- Greer noted: "If we are ahead of the game, like we are on AI chips, sometimes they feel that can stop their own growth"
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has long warned this shift was coming and would erode US influence

(source: Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md)

## Implications

The bifurcation of AI hardware into US-allied and China-domestic ecosystems appears to be accelerating. Nvidia risks permanently losing access to the Chinese market — one of the largest potential markets for AI compute. (source: Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md)
