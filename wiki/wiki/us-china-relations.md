# US-China Relations

**Summary**: The US-China relationship in 2026 is characterized by fragile stability, with China increasingly meeting US pressure with deterrence rather than concessions, and both nations navigating tensions over Taiwan, trade, technology, and the Iran conflict.

**Sources**: What did Donald Trump achieve in talks with Xi Jinping?.md, Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-16

## Current State (May 2026)

The relationship operates under what Xi Jinping has termed "new constructive strategic stability" — a framework announced at the [[trump-xi-summit-may-2026]] that aims to keep competition within proper limits and ensure predictable peace. Analysts interpret this as China-imposed guardrails on US behaviour. (source: What did Donald Trump achieve in talks with Xi Jinping?.md)

## Shift in Power Dynamics

The negotiating dynamic has fundamentally shifted since Trump's first term:

- **Previously**: US demands and pressure; China manages with concessions
- **Now**: China handles the US by deterrence rather than concessions
- China only makes concessions in exchange for US concessions
- China has proved willing to meet escalation with escalation

Key moment: In 2025, Trump backed down on tariffs after Chinese retaliation triggered a market backlash and Beijing imposed rare earth export controls that alarmed Washington. Trump has since sought to avoid new escalation. (source: What did Donald Trump achieve in talks with Xi Jinping?.md)

## Key Flashpoints

### Taiwan
Xi warned that "mishandling" of Taiwan could result in conflict between the two powers. Trump has not decided on a $14 billion arms sale to Taiwan. Xi's framework includes "deep red lines on Taiwan." (source: What did Donald Trump achieve in talks with Xi Jinping?.md)

### Technology and Chips
The US approved Nvidia's H200 for sale to China in December 2025, but Beijing has not permitted purchases, instead pushing [[china-chip-self-sufficiency]]. Chip export controls were notably not discussed at the May 2026 summit. (source: Nvidia's Future in China Remains Unclear After Trump-Xi Summit.md)

### Iran and Energy
The US-Israel war against Iran and the resulting rise in global energy prices form a key backdrop. Trump sought Chinese help pressuring Iran on the Strait of Hormuz but received no public commitment. Trump is considering lifting sanctions on Chinese purchasers of Iranian oil as a concession. (source: What did Donald Trump achieve in talks with Xi Jinping?.md)
