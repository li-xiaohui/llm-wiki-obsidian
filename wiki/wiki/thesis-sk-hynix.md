# Investment Thesis: SK Hynix (000660.KS)

**Summary**: Long SK Hynix — the $860B HBM monopoly with 62% market share, 70% of Nvidia's HBM4 orders, record 72% operating margins, and a forward P/E of ~6x. US ADR listing in H2 2026 is the next major catalyst.

**Sources**: SK Hynix Investment Thesis - Long.md, South Korea's world-beating stock rally stumbles as global funds sell.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-16

---

## Direction: LONG | Conviction: HIGH

## Elevator Pitch

SK Hynix controls 62% of HBM — the most critical bottleneck in AI infrastructure. It holds 70% of Nvidia's HBM4 orders for Vera Rubin, posted 72% operating margins (global chip industry record), and is priced at ~6x forward earnings. Customer demand exceeds supply through 2028. A US ADR listing ($10-14B) in H2 2026 unlocks a new investor base.

## HBM Monopoly Position

- **62% market share** (Q2 2025), 53% (Q3 2025 as Samsung recovered)
- **70% of Nvidia's HBM4** orders for Vera Rubin platform
- **Sold out** entire 2026 HBM supply
- **Demand > supply** through 2028 per management
- HBM4 mass production underway; HBM4E targeted for 2027
- Also supplying Microsoft Maia 200 custom chip
- Pricing power: ~20% HBM3E price hikes planned, DRAM +47% in 2026

## US ADR Listing (H2 2026)

- Confidentially filed Form F-1 with SEC
- Targeting $10-14 billion raise
- Will unlock US retail/institutional access (currently hard to buy for most US investors)
- Plan: issue new ADR shares while buying back/cancelling existing shares

## Capital Allocation

- 50% of accumulated FCF returned to shareholders
- Annual dividend raised 25% to KRW 1,500/share
- Expected shareholder returns in 2026: KRW 45-90T
- Targeting >KRW 100T net cash position
- $15B fab investment + $12.85B packaging plant + $3.87B Indiana HBM4E/5 facility

## Key Risks

| Risk | Severity | Assessment |
|------|----------|------------|
| Samsung HBM4 catch-up | HIGH | Passed Nvidia/AMD validation, full supply from June. Could take share from 70% to 50% |
| 2027-2028 oversupply | HIGH | SK Hynix quietly cut HBM capex in 2025 on internal models. Capex today = wafers in 2027 |
| Pure-play concentration | MEDIUM | ~95% memory revenue. No diversification buffer |
| Kospi volatility | MEDIUM | -7.7% on May 15 alone. 17% of daily volume from ETF rebalancing |
| HBM commoditization | MEDIUM | If HBM becomes commodity, margins compress from 72% to 30-40% |

2. Samsung secures >50% of Nvidia's HBM4 orders
3. DRAM spot prices decline 3 consecutive months
4. SK Hynix loses HBM4E/HBM5 first-mover to Samsung
