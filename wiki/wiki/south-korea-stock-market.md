# South Korea Stock Market

**Summary**: South Korea's Kospi index staged a near-90% rally in 2025-2026 driven by two chipmakers (Samsung and SK Hynix), but extreme concentration and foreign selling have made it the most volatile major index in the world.

**Sources**: South Korea's world-beating stock rally stumbles as global funds sell.md, Global Funds Ramp Up Korea Stock Exit as Record Rally Extends.md, Japan and South Korea tech stocks swoon and bond yields soar.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-16

---

## The Rally

The Kospi Index rose nearly 90% through mid-May 2026, with Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix accounting for two-thirds of the gains. The index briefly hit the 8,000 milestone on May 15, 2026, triggering celebration in trading rooms before reversing sharply. (source: South Korea's world-beating stock rally stumbles as global funds sell.md)

## The Crash on May 15, 2026

The Kospi dropped 488.23 points (6.12%) to close at 7,493.18 on May 15:

- Samsung Electronics: -8.6%
- SK Hynix: -7.7%
- Intra-day drop reached 7.6% (biggest since March 9)
- Korea Exchange briefly halted programme selling after futures fell 5%+
- The exchange cancelled its planned 8,000 milestone celebration

No single catalyst triggered the drop beyond ongoing concerns over Samsung's labour dispute. The sell-off was described as "exhaustion after a rally that came too fast, too hard." (source: South Korea's world-beating stock rally stumbles as global funds sell.md)

## Foreign Fund Exodus

Foreign investors sold 5.6 trillion won ($4.9 billion) of Kospi shares on May 15 alone, bringing the week's net withdrawal to 14.3 trillion won. Global funds have deepened their retreat even as the market climbed on local inflows and the AI boom. (source: South Korea's world-beating stock rally stumbles as global funds sell.md, Global Funds Ramp Up Korea Stock Exit as Record Rally Extends.md)

## Structural Vulnerabilities

- Samsung and SK Hynix account for nearly half of the Kospi Index
- Leveraged ETFs tracking these stocks are hugely popular, causing rebalancing-driven swings
- ~17% of SK Hynix daily volume and ~10% of Samsung volume comes from ETF rebalancing
- Retail investors are borrowing record sums to amplify bets
- Daily gyrations of 5%+ have become common
- Kospi 200 Volatility Index is at a record high versus the VIX
- Cost of downside protection at highest since March

The Kospi is "expensive relative to Korea's own history" and has become "a bet on two large memory semiconductor companies." (source: South Korea's world-beating stock rally stumbles as global funds sell.md)

## Samsung Labour Dispute

Samsung's labour union indicated negotiations would resume only after June 7, signalling an extended strike. Samsung urged the union to resume dialogue over bonus payments. This accelerated selling in Samsung shares. (source: South Korea's world-beating stock rally stumbles as global funds sell.md)

## Regional Context

The sell-off occurred alongside broader Asian tech weakness. Rising oil prices from the [[iran-oil-crisis-2026]], lack of meaningful outcomes from the [[trump-xi-summit-may-2026]], and Trump's remarks about not needing the Strait of Hormuz to open all weighed on sentiment. Higher bond yields also hurt risk appetite. (source: Japan and South Korea tech stocks swoon and bond yields soar.md, South Korea's world-beating stock rally stumbles as global funds sell.md)
